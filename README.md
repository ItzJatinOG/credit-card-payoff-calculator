# Credit Card Payoff Calculator

A free, fast credit card payoff calculator. Enter your balance, APR and monthly payment to see your **debt-free date**, **total interest** and how much a higher payment would save.

**Live site:** [https://YOUR-DOMAIN.com](https://itzjatinog.github.io/credit-card-payoff-calculator/

## What it does

- Shows how many months it takes to pay off a credit card
- Calculates total interest and total amount paid
- Works backwards from a goal date to the monthly payment you need
- Shows the interest saved by paying 20% more each month
- Gives a year-by-year payoff breakdown
- Supports US dollars (USD), Indian rupees (INR) and British pounds (GBP)
- Light and dark mode, mobile friendly, no sign-up

## How it works

Each month, interest is the balance multiplied by the APR divided by 12. Your payment covers that interest first, and the rest reduces the balance. The calculator repeats this month by month until the balance reaches zero.

Assumptions: fixed APR, fixed payment, no new purchases, no fees. Results are estimates and not financial advice.

## Privacy

All calculations run in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to a server or stored.

## Tech

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript in a single `index.html` file. No frameworks, no build step, no dependencies. Hosted on GitHub Pages.
